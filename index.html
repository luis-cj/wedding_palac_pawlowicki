Flights

Fly into Wrocław (WRO) if you can — it's a small, easy airport and it puts you 30 minutes from the palace.

From Spain

Low-cost airlines fly to Wrocław from several Spanish cities, though not every day. If nothing lines up with the Friday, look at Kraków or Warsaw and take a train from there.

From London

There are direct flights from London to Wrocław, usually from Luton or Stansted, and the flight takes a little over two hours.

Other airports

Kraków, Poznań, Katowice, Warsaw and Berlin all connect to Wrocław by train or coach in two to five hours. Worth checking if the direct flights are expensive.

When to book

July is high season in Poland and prices climb as the date gets closer. Book as soon as you've sent your RSVP.

From the airport to the palace

The airport is on the south-west edge of the city and the palace is on the north-east side, so you cross Wrocław to get there — about 25 km.

Taxi, Bolt or UberEasiest

Around 40 minutes door to door, and roughly 90–120 PLN (about €25). Both apps work well in Wrocław, and taxis wait right outside arrivals.

Bus and tram

Bus 106 runs from outside the terminal to the main station every 15 minutes, then you change for the ride out to Pawłowice. Cheap, but closer to an hour and a half with luggage.

Hire car

Only worth it if you're travelling around Poland before or after. There is parking at the palace.

Money

Poland uses the złoty, not the euro. Cards work almost everywhere.

Bus tickets

Buy on board with a contactless card, or from the machine at the stop.

Getting around

The jakdojade app plans any journey in the city and sells tickets.

Time zone

Same as Spain, one hour ahead of the UK.

Stay at the palace with us

There are rooms at the venue itself, and it's by far the simplest option: no taxis late at night, breakfast with everyone else, and you're already where the party is. You're very welcome to book somewhere in the city instead if you'd rather — just tell us on the RSVP so we know who needs a room.

Prefer to stay in the city?

Completely fine — plenty of guests will. Wrocław's old town has hotels at every price, and it's about 25 minutes from the palace by taxi. Just choose "no" on the RSVP so we don't hold a room you won't use, and plan how you'll get back after the party.
